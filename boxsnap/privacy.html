BoxSnap – Privacy Policy

Jookata · com.jookata.ghihinhban

BoxSnap does not collect your videos or order data: they stay on your phone and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. The only data that leaves your phone goes to Google (Drive, sign-in and ads), as described below.

Effective date: 31 October 2026. App: BoxSnap (package com.jookata.ghihinhban), published by Jookata on Google Play.

1. Who we are

BoxSnap is developed by Jookata ("we"). BoxSnap helps online sellers record the packing of each order as evidence for customer or marketplace disputes. We do not run any server that receives your data.

QR codes and hand signs are recognised entirely on your phone, using Google ML Kit and Google MediaPipe models built into the app. Camera images are never uploaded for recognition.

Order slips show customer names, phone numbers and addresses. These details may appear in your videos. You are responsible for how you store and share them. We cannot see them.

3. Google Drive (optional)

  • If you connect Google Drive, you sign in with Google. Google gives the app a temporary access token; we never see your password.
  • The app only asks for the drive.file permission. It can only see and manage files and folders that BoxSnap itself creates. It cannot read your other Drive files.
  • The app uploads your videos to a "BoxSnap" folder in your Drive, searches those videos, and moves them to Trash when you ask.
  • When you tap "Send Google Drive link", the app makes that one video viewable by anyone with the link. The app asks you first.
  • Uploads go directly from your phone to Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • You can disconnect in Settings, or remove BoxSnap's access at any time in your Google Account (Security → Third-party apps with account access).

Google's handling of Drive data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

BoxSnap's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • BoxSnap shows ads from Google AdMob: an app-open ad, a full-screen ad when you leave the Find videos screen, and a banner on that screen. No ads appear on the recording screen.
  • Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and ad interactions to show, measure and personalise ads, and to prevent fraud.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice in Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Google Play services and ML Kit

Google sign-in uses Google Play services. ML Kit may contact Google to receive model updates and to send anonymous metrics about API performance and usage. It does not send camera images.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your data. We receive none of it. Data reaches third parties only when you choose it: uploading to your Google Drive, or sending a video or link with another app.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Videos and the order log stay on your phone until you delete them. You can delete single videos, clean up old videos, or auto-delete phone copies already on Drive (default: after 60 days).
  • Videos in your Google Drive stay there until you delete them in the app or in Google Drive.
  • Uninstalling BoxSnap removes the order log and settings. Videos already saved in the Gallery and in Google Drive are not removed.

9. Children

BoxSnap is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Security

Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox and your screen lock. Transfers to Google use HTTPS. No method is 100% secure; protect your phone and Google account.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

3. Google Drive (không bắt buộc)

  • Khi kết nối Google Drive, bạn đăng nhập bằng Google. Google cấp cho ứng dụng một mã truy cập tạm thời; chúng tôi không bao giờ thấy mật khẩu của bạn.
  • Ứng dụng chỉ xin quyền drive.file. Ứng dụng chỉ thấy và quản lý được các tệp, thư mục do chính BoxSnap tạo, không đọc được các tệp khác trong Drive của bạn.
  • Ứng dụng tải video lên thư mục "BoxSnap" trong Drive của bạn, tìm các video đó, và chuyển chúng vào Thùng rác khi bạn yêu cầu.
  • Khi bạn bấm "Gửi link Google Drive", ứng dụng cho phép bất kỳ ai có đường link xem đúng video đó. Ứng dụng hỏi bạn trước khi làm.
  • Video được tải thẳng từ điện thoại lên Google qua kết nối mã hoá (HTTPS).
  • Bạn có thể ngắt kết nối trong Cài đặt, hoặc gỡ quyền của BoxSnap bất cứ lúc nào trong Tài khoản Google (Bảo mật → Ứng dụng bên thứ ba có quyền truy cập).

Việc Google xử lý dữ liệu Drive tuân theo Chính sách quyền riêng tư của Google.

Việc BoxSnap sử dụng và chuyển giao thông tin nhận được từ Google API tuân thủ Chính sách dữ liệu người dùng của Google API Services, bao gồm các yêu cầu về Giới hạn sử dụng (Limited Use).
