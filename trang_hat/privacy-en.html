Privacy Policy — Mala Prayer Beads

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the “Mala Prayer Beads” app (package com.jookata.tranghat), published by Jookata (“we”), handles your information. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

The app has no account or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or photos. We do not operate any server that receives your data.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following data is kept in the app's private storage on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Bead counts and your daily practice calendar;
  • Settings: wooden fish tempo, beads per round, sound, vibration, selected image;
  • The playback position of the mantra or sutra audio;
  • MP3 files you choose to add (copied into the app's private storage).

If Android backup is turned on, the system may include this data in the backup of your Google account, according to Google's settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: an App Open ad when you open or return to the app, and a small banner on the practice calendar screen. No ads are shown on the bead counting screen.

To serve, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • The device's advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and app information (model, Android version, language);
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks).

How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites — Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Consent and your choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and wherever the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent form (UMP) before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → “Privacy options (ads)”.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalized ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

5. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback when counting beads.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and notifications: to keep playing the mantra when you leave the app, with a notification that has a pause button.
  • Wake lock: so audio does not stop when the screen turns off.
  • File selection: the app uses the system file picker and reads only the file you choose. It does not scan your storage.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us.

7. Data retention and deletion

All app data stays on your device. You can delete a track from the Audio list, reset the counter, or remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. For advertising data processed by Google, please use the Google tools described in section 4.

8. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage, protected by the Android sandbox. Ad connections are encrypted over HTTPS by the Google SDK.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
